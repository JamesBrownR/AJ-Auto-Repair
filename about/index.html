Our Shop

A Shop That Works for You

AJ Automotive Services is a full-service auto repair shop located at 1534 Kurt St in Eustis, Florida. We work on all makes and models, foreign and domestic, covering everything from routine oil changes and brake service to transmission repair, electrical diagnostics, engine work, and custom builds.

We built this shop on a straightforward principle: diagnose the vehicle first, give you a clear estimate, and let you decide before any work starts. No repairs you did not approve, no parts swapped by guesswork, no inflated bills at pickup. That is how we operate every day, for every customer.

We serve Eustis and all of Lake County including Mount Dora, Tavares, Leesburg, Fruitland Park, Umatilla, and Lady Lake. Open Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM.

The Owners

Andrew & Jeremy

AJ Automotive is owned and operated by Andrew and Jeremy, who work in the shop directly. When you bring your vehicle in, the people diagnosing it and making recommendations are the same people with their names on the door.

Both have hands-on experience across a wide range of makes and models including domestic trucks, Japanese imports, European vehicles, classics, motorcycles, ATVs, and marine engines. That range matters because it means we can handle what most shops turn away.

The shop stays small by design. Every vehicle gets the owners' attention, not whoever happens to be available. That is not something a larger operation can offer.
