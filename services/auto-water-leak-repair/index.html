At AJ Automotive Services in Eustis, Florida, we diagnose and repair water leaks on all makes and models, foreign and domestic. Water intrusion is one of the more frustrating problems a vehicle can develop because the source is often not where the water appears. Water that soaks a rear floor mat may be entering from a windshield seal, a door seal, a clogged drain, or a body seam feet away from where you notice it.

Florida makes water leaks more common and more damaging than in drier climates. The combination of heavy rainfall, high humidity, and intense heat accelerates seal degradation, breeds mold quickly in wet carpet and padding, and corrodes electrical connectors that get wet repeatedly. A leak that seems minor today can produce a serious mold problem or electrical fault within weeks if it is not addressed.

We start with a systematic diagnosis to find the actual entry point before recommending any repair. Common sources include deteriorated door and window seals, clogged sunroof drains, failed windshield or rear glass seals, deteriorated firewall grommets, and blocked HVAC drain lines. We tell you exactly what we find and what the repair involves before any work begins.

We serve Eustis, Mount Dora, Tavares, Leesburg, Lady Lake, Fruitland Park, Umatilla, and all of Lake County. Open Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
