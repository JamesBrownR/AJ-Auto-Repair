5 Warning Signs Your Brakes Need Immediate Attention

  • Squealing, grinding, or scraping noises when braking
  • Soft, spongy, or low brake pedal that sinks to the floor
  • Vehicle pulls to one side when braking
  • Vibration or pulsation in the brake pedal or steering wheel
  • Brake warning light illuminated on dashboard

If you experience any of these symptoms, schedule a brake inspection immediately.
