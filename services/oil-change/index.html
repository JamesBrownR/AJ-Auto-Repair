Engine oil lubricates moving parts, reduces friction, pulls heat away from the engine, and carries contaminants to the filter. Over time and mileage it breaks down and becomes less effective. Florida's heat accelerates that process. Regular oil changes are the single most cost-effective thing you can do to extend your engine's life and avoid repairs that cost ten times more.
