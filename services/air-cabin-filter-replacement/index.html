At AJ Automotive Services in Eustis, Florida, we replace engine air filters and cabin air filters on all makes and models, foreign and domestic. Both filters are simple services that make a real difference when they are done on time and a noticeable problem when they are ignored too long.

The engine air filter keeps dirt, dust, pollen, and debris out of your engine. A clogged filter restricts airflow to the engine, which hurts performance, reduces fuel economy, and over time puts stress on engine components. Florida roads kick up a lot of dust and pollen, and vehicles driven in Lake County often need their engine air filters replaced more frequently than the manufacturer's general interval would suggest.

The cabin air filter cleans the air that comes through your vents into the passenger compartment. A dirty cabin filter reduces airflow through the HVAC system, makes your A/C and heat less effective, and lets dust, pollen, and mold spores circulate in the cabin. In Florida's humid conditions, a long-overdue cabin filter can develop odors as well.

We check both filters whenever we have a vehicle in for any service and tell you honestly where they stand. Neither replacement takes long, and we use the correct filter for your specific vehicle every time.
