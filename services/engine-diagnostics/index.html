Why Regular Oil Changes Save You Money

What Oil Does

Engine oil serves four critical functions:

  • Lubricates moving parts
  • Cools the engine
  • Cleans internal components
  • Seals gaps between pistons and cylinders

What Happens When You Skip Changes

Old, dirty oil causes:

  • Increased friction and wear
  • Sludge buildup
  • Reduced engine performance
  • Eventually, complete engine failure

The Cost Comparison

Simple math shows prevention wins:

  • Oil change: $50-$80
  • Engine rebuild: $3,000-$5,000
  • Engine replacement: $4,000-$7,000
  • Skip oil changes = destroy engine

Recommended Maintenance Schedule

Service Interval Why It Matters
Oil Change (Conventional) Every 3,000-5,000 miles Prevents engine wear and sludge buildup
Oil Change (Synthetic) Every 7,000-10,000 miles Better protection, longer intervals
Air Filter Every 12,000-15,000 miles Maintains airflow and fuel economy
Spark Plugs Every 30,000-100,000 miles Ensures proper combustion and performance
Coolant Flush Every 30,000-50,000 miles Prevents overheating and corrosion
Timing Belt Every 60,000-100,000 miles Prevents catastrophic engine failure
