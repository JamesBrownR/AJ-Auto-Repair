At AJ Automotive Services in Eustis, Florida, we diagnose and repair engines on all makes and models, foreign and domestic. Engine problems range from minor issues like a faulty sensor or a small oil leak to major internal failures. We start with an accurate diagnosis so you know exactly what you are dealing with before any decisions are made.

We scan for fault codes, interpret what they actually mean for your specific vehicle, and perform the diagnostic work needed to confirm the root cause rather than just replacing parts until something works.

For major engine work, we give you a straight assessment of whether a repair makes sense for the value of the vehicle. We have seen plenty of engines that were worth rebuilding and plenty that were not. We will tell you honestly which situation you are in.
