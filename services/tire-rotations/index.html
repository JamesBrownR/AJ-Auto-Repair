At AJ Automotive Services in Eustis, Florida, we perform tire rotations on all makes and models, foreign and domestic. A tire rotation is one of the simplest and most cost-effective services you can do for your vehicle. Moving tires to different positions on the vehicle equalizes the wear they experience and significantly extends the life of the set.

Florida roads and driving conditions put specific demands on tires. The heat accelerates rubber degradation, and the mix of highway driving, stop-and-go traffic, and the occasional flooded roadway means your tires need to be in good condition year round. When we rotate your tires we also inspect each one for uneven wear patterns, low tread depth, sidewall damage, and inflation, so you know exactly where your tires stand.

We serve Eustis, Mount Dora, Tavares, Leesburg, Lady Lake, Fruitland Park, Umatilla, and all of Lake County. Open Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM.

Check your owner's manual for the specific recommendation for your vehicle. If you drive aggressively, carry heavy loads regularly, or notice uneven wear developing, rotating more frequently makes sense. - question: Does it matter which tires go where? answer: Yes. The correct rotation pattern depends on your vehicle's drivetrain and whether your tires are directional or non-directional. Front-wheel drive, rear-wheel drive, and all-wheel drive vehicles each wear their tires differently and use different patterns. Directional tires can only move front to back on the same side. We use the correct pattern for your specific vehicle every time. - question: Can I skip tire rotations if my tires look fine? answer: Uneven wear develops gradually and is not always obvious until it is well advanced. By the time you can clearly see a tire wearing differently, it has already lost significant tread life on the affected areas. Regular rotations prevent the uneven wear from developing in the first place rather than trying to correct it after the fact. - question: Do tire rotations really extend tire life? answer: Yes, meaningfully. Front tires on a front-wheel drive vehicle handle steering, braking, and power delivery simultaneously, which wears them much faster than the rears. Without rotation, front tires often wear out in half the miles of the rears, meaning you replace two tires instead of all four at the same time. Regular rotation keeps all four tires wearing at a similar rate so you replace the full set together at maximum mileage. - question: Should I get an alignment at the same time as a rotation? answer: Not necessarily every time, but alignment should be checked if we find uneven wear during the rotation, if the vehicle is pulling, if you have recently hit a large pothole or curb, or if it has been more than a year since the last alignment check. We will tell you honestly whether we see signs that suggest an alignment is needed. - question: Do you service all makes and models? answer: Yes.
